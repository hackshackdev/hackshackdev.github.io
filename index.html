Ground Zero

A wave-based FPS, where up to 8 players raid an alien planet to capture and defend "Forges". The players must fend off the alien androids and cyborg animals while hacking the forge, and using the resources (and their enemies) to their advantage.

Pre-alpha Release: January 1, 2025
